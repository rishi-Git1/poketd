POKE BASTION — VERSION 28 FORMS, EVERSTONE & EXPEDITION REVIEW
Requires Version 27. ONLY changed/new files are included.

INSTALL
1. Extract into your existing project root beside package.json.
2. Merge folders and replace matching files. Keep all other assets.
3. Redeploy Render: npm run build; publish dist.
4. Refresh and confirm VERSION 28.

FINAL BOSSES
All scripted final encounters have 3x their Version-27 HP and 2x their Version-27 movement speed. Includes every regional finale and Kanto's Mewtwo/Mew sequence. Existing story-boss health and post-100 scaling remain. Other boss stats, normal gym/League encounters and random Endless bosses keep their previous multipliers. Regional Endless retains its boosted story finales.

LOSS REVIEW
Losing opens You Whited Out! with the reached round, defeats, upgrades, final HP and party. Review expedition returns to the normal party/dossier view, exiting fullscreen when necessary. Party damage totals, contributions, form history and inventory remain available; combat and reward spending stay stopped. Review expedition summary reopens the record. Start another expedition or choose a region when ready. The current record stays in memory until a new run or reload; it is not an archive across reloads.

HOTKEYS
N: start next round when ready.
Enter: also starts the next round while the page body has focus.
A: toggle Auto.
S: cycle 1x / 2x / 5x game speed.
Space: pause/resume; 1/2/3: choose a reward.
Text/search fields, selects, editable content, open dialogs, modifiers, composition and repeated keydowns do not trigger gameplay shortcuts. N/Enter do not discard a lost expedition.

FORM ITEMS AND ABILITIES
Eligible partners unlock 35 form-item/ability families from round 1. Includes Reveal Glass for all four genies, Rotom Catalog, Deoxys Meteorite, Gracidea, Origin-form items, DNA Splicers, Prison Bottle, Primal Orbs, crowned weapons, Calyrex Reins of Unity, both Darmanitan Zen Modes, Schooling, Battle Bond, Stance Change, Forecast, Palafin Hero, and more. Arceus Plates, Silvally Memories, Genesect Drives and Cherrim Sunshine add 39 typed/appearance form variants.

Necrozma uses N-Solarizer for Dusk Mane or N-Lunarizer for Dawn Wings. Only those fused forms unlock Ultranecrozium Z for Ultra Burst. Each step spends a separate reward. Fusion items provide the form without consuming another party member. Ability/weather/battle forms persist for the run as roguelike adaptations.

Select the item, choose a partner/form, or cancel/Escape to retain the same unspent roll. Each new form retains its stage, party buffs and individual damage/contributions, uses its form stats and a real associated move, and adds x1.25 personal damage. Each individual may visit each destination once; used destinations cannot waste another pick. Personal power survives later eligible Mega/Gigantamax/Tera changes. Alternate upgrade forms are not ordinary enemies or recruits. Regional forms are added only for species already in that region. Galarian Standard Darmanitan remains its ordinary evolution target. Type interactions remain entirely neutral.

EVERSTONE
Appears from round 1 only when a partner has an evolution or available/future regional form change. Choose one holder to permanently block its evolution, alternate forms, Mega, Gigantamax and Tera choices. It keeps its present form, stats, damage and power. Other partners remain eligible. Blocked cards refresh out of banked choices and future rolls. A shared item may still appear for another eligible partner. Everstone cannot be repeated on the same holder or exchanged as a passive buff; a new/replacement partner does not inherit it. Party, dossier and inventory show the lock.
