STORY BOSSES AND DIFFICULTY
All scripted boss teams across all nine regions have another x2 maximum HP: gyms/Totems, League/Champion Cup/Ultra Beast teams, champions, Mewtwo and every regional finale. Previous boss boosts remain. Random Endless and True Endless bosses do not get this extra boss-health multiplier.

From round 101, story enemies gain another x1.02 HP, physical/special defense and tower impact per round, plus a linear 0.5% movement increase per round. At round 150 this adds x2.692 to HP/defenses/impact and x1.25 speed. Kanto continues through 151. Regional Endless retains the story changes and freezes this extra ramp at the finale; its later 5% combat-stat and linear 2% speed scaling continues. True Endless scaling is unchanged.

ROUND-100 FIELD HAZARDS
Stealth Rock: Sinnoh onward; max 1 pick; regular enemies have 12.5% less HP.
Spikes: Johto onward; max 3 picks; regular enemies have 6.25%, 12.5%, then 25% less HP in total at each tier.
Toxic Spikes: Sinnoh onward; max 2 picks; regular enemies lose 0.25%, then 0.50% of their maximum HP per game second.
True Endless allows all three, from round 100.

Stealth Rock and Spikes multiply together, leaving 65.625% regular enemy HP at full stacks. Taking one midwave proportionally updates current/max HP of regular enemies already present. Toxic Spikes uses maximum HP after those cuts, ignores all types/defenses, and follows simulation speed. Every scripted and random boss is exempt from all hazards.

Once capped, hazards cannot appear in new or already banked rolls. Owned counts refresh as stacks are selected. Hazard cards never become master versions. These permanent installations remain for the run and cannot be exchanged. Repeated Spikes/Toxic Spikes still give the existing repeat-resonance bonus. Inventory and party support show stack totals and effects. Hazard damage is kept separate from individual Pokemon totals; poison kills count toward the run and global defeat healing.

ART
Each hazard has miniature card art. Every installation adds six ground instances, with additional stacks adding more art while existing instances stay in place. Ground art persists across waves and fullscreen and resets with a new run/region.
