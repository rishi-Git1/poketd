POKÉ BASTION VERSION30 — WEATHER, TERRAIN, ZONES

INSTALL OVER VERSION29
Extract into your existing project root, merge folders and replace matching files. Keep other files/assets. Source and prebuilt dist are included. This is a changed-files patch, not a full replacement project.
Redeploy on Render with build command npm run build and publish directory dist. Confirm VERSION30 in the header. No new dependencies or remote assets are required.

18 FIELDS, ONE PER TYPE
Weather: Sunny (Fire), Rain (Water).
Terrain: Electric Terrain, Grassy Terrain, Psychic Terrain.
Zones: Normal, Fighting, Flying, Poison, Ground, Rock, Bug, Ghost, Steel, Ice, Dragon, Dark and Fairy Zone.
No Sandstorm, Hail, Misty Terrain or invented Stellar field.

ROUND25+ — GENERATION3 AND LATER
Available in Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar and Paldea, including their Endless modes. True Endless also allows WTZ from25. Kanto and Johto do not offer WTZ.
A normal WTZ permanently gives matching-type friendly attacks 1.5-times damage. Initial offers match a current party attack. Its whole eligible family has the selection weight of one Silvally Memory card, not one weight per type. At most one WTZ appears in a three-card roll.

ONE PERMANENT FIELD
Choosing a field locks the expedition to that WTZ. Every other normal or EX field disappears from banked and future choices. Only the matching EX can appear afterward. WTZ does not add repeat resonance or turn into random Master upgrades. The field stays installed if party members change; only matching attacks get its boost.

ROUND100+ — EX REWARDS AND PREMIUM EXCHANGES
EX WTZ can appear in ordinary rewards and premium upgrade trades from100, with the combined selection weight of one Mega Evolution card. It is optional, never guaranteed.
EX replaces normal's 1.5-times boost with 3-times TOTAL damage, not4.5-times. If no field has been chosen, an eligible EX may be selected directly and locks that field. If a normal field is owned, only its EX can be offered. Once EX is installed, all WTZ rewards stop.
WTZ installations are permanent and cannot be traded away as source upgrades. A premium EX exchange consumes one selected ordinary passive upgrade, retaining the separate Pokémon exchange and category reroll rights. Stale choices are checked before taking an upgrade or spending an exchange; invalid cached offers refresh without charging a reroll.

APPEARANCE AND COMBAT
Every field changes the entire battlefield with its own palette and scenery: sunlight rays, rain/cloud bands, branching electrical traces, sprouts, psychic prisms, silver stars, fighting slashes, wind ribbons, toxic haze, earth cracks, stone facets, honeycomb/insect wings, ghost wisps, metal plates, ice crystals, dragon crests, shadow bands and fairy stars/bows.
EX intensifies that field's scenery. No WTZ circles, radial areas, damage ticks, healing ticks, enemy-stat changes or damage hitboxes are added. Existing map/attack/hazard behavior stays separate. The live field/type/multiplier badge works in fullscreen. The dossier and collected upgrades show the current multiplier and normal-to-EX replacement.
WTZ follows the actual friendly attack type, including Z-Moves, Tera and form signatures. Projectiles/bounces keep their recorded move type. Other upgrades compound normally. Enemy typing never changes targeting or damage: no weaknesses, resistances or immunities return.
