ENDLESS MOVEMENT SPEED
During the extra scaling phase, speed gains a fixed 2% of the phase-start baseline each round. It does not compound. The baseline remains fixed so ordinary wave growth cannot make the result quadratic.

After 10 scaling rounds: 1.20x baseline speed.
After 50 scaling rounds: 2.00x baseline speed.
After 100 scaling rounds: 3.00x baseline speed.

True Endless starts counting at round 1 using ordinary round-1 speed as the baseline. Regional Endless keeps its existing speed curve through round 151, then gains 2% of its round-151 baseline per additional round (1.02x at round 152).

HP, physical/special defenses and impact damage retain their existing 5% compounding and other growth. Boss speed multipliers still apply. Classic mode and regional Endless before the extra scaling phase are unchanged. Type-independent targeting from Version 24 remains included in the existing project.
