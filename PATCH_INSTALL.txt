POKÉ BASTION VERSION 29 — ALOLA Z-MOVES

INSTALL OVER VERSION 28
Extract this ZIP into your existing project root (where package.json lives), merge folders and replace matching files. Keep other project files and assets. Source and prebuilt dist are included; this is a changed-files patch, not a full game ZIP.

On Render, keep build command npm run build and publish directory dist. Redeploy and confirm VERSION 29 in the header. A hard refresh may be needed for a previously open page. No dependency installation or runtime asset downloads are required.

ALOLA ONLY
Z-Crystals unlock from round 25 in Alola Classic and regional Endless. They do not appear in other regions or True Endless. The whole eligible crystal pool has the same selection weight as one Mega Stone / Max Mushrooms reward. At most one Z-Crystal is offered per roll. Pick a compatible partner; cancel preserves all three choices and the reward queue. Auto continues during selection.

35 OFFICIAL Z-MOVES
All 18 type crystals plus 17 signature crystals are included. Generic crystals require a shared holder type. Signature crystals require their official species/form, including Alolan Raichu, cap Pikachu, the Tapus and the correct Necrozma fusion. Mew and Partner Cap Pikachu are added as Alola signature-holder exceptions. The displayed regional dex count still represents the official regional list.

Damaging crystals replace each attack volley with a stronger, larger Z-Move, using its official name and a new authored animation. They do not increase base stats, personal form power or collected buff multipliers. Enhanced moves have at least 2.4 times native move power (minimum 7.2, or 8.8 for exclusives), and wider area/beam/cone, five-shot volley or six-enemy chain where applicable. Normal combat cadence, conditional tactics, splash/ricochets and actual damage contribution tracking all apply. Type interactions remain completely neutral.

BOOSTING EXCEPTIONS
Extreme Evoboost and Clangorous Soulblaze give their individual holder exactly 300 times personal damage while compatible and held. Extreme Evoboost empowers Eevee's ordinary attack; Clangorous Soulblaze also deals enhanced area damage. The boost is applied once and does not accumulate on re-equipping. Replacing the crystal, or evolving out of its eligible holder, removes that effect.

HELD ITEMS AND MEGAS
One crystal per partner. A different crystal replaces the old move/boost; the same crystal cannot be selected again for its current holder. Already Mega-Evolved Pokemon cannot hold crystals. Crystal holders cannot Mega Evolve. Stale banked cards repair immediately. Evolution/form changes retain the crystal; if incompatible it becomes dormant until replaced. Everstone permits attack-only crystals while still locking evolution/forms. Partner trades do not transfer a held item to the replacement.

NECROZMA
Necrozma -> N-Solarizer or N-Lunarizer -> Dusk Mane or Dawn Wings -> hold Ultranecrozium Z -> choose a separate Ultra Burst form reward -> Ultra Necrozma.
Ultra Burst requires the actual held crystal and is Alola-only. The crystal itself gives no stat boost. It unlocks Light That Burns the Sky only in Ultra form. Without that active crystal, Ultra Necrozma's ordinary move is Photon Geyser. Existing form upgrades still grant their separate 1.25-times form power and preserve evolution stage/contributions.

INTERFACE AND ART
Held crystals show in the party, detailed dossier and upgrade inventory. Alola's Help -> Attack animation guide previews all 35 Z-Moves without equipping anything. Animations include a giant fire sun, waterspout, blooming flowers, acid rain, silk cocoon, spectral hands, monolith, ice prison, rainbow thunder, clanging scales, wrestling ring and Ultra Necrozma's prismatic sky columns.
