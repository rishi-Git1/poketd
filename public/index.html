FIELD GUIDE

Hold the clearing.

Choose a starter, then start a wave. Your party automatically fires at enemies moving toward the tower. Each teammate uses its primary type; standard type advantages apply.

Enemies arrive in overlapping groups of three from all directions. HP, defense, speed, and impact damage derive from their base stats. Stage 2 enemies join at wave 4; stage 3 at wave 9.

Survive a wave to choose one of three rewards: recruit, evolve, improve the tower, or strengthen a type or evolution stage. Build a party of up to six. Damage carries between waves; the Repair & Reinforce card restores health.

Space: pause · 1 / 2 / 3: choose a card · Enter: start wave. Leaving the tab pauses combat. Your best wave is saved on this device.
